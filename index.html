Common questions

How do I convert my TU percentage to a US GPA?

Enter each course with its credits and mark, and pick the grading scale printed on your transcript. Each course is converted to a US letter grade, then averaged by credits to give a GPA on the 4.0 or 4.3 scale.

Which grading scale should I choose for BSc CSIT?

Choose the scale that matches the grading table on your transcript. For Tribhuvan University BSc CSIT, the transcript lists First Division as 70% and above, which is the “Tribhuvan University Undergraduate (Pass at 40)” scale.

Is this GPA accepted by US universities?

It is an unofficial estimate for application forms that ask you to self-report a GPA. Universities that require an official evaluation, such as WES, calculate their own GPA, which may differ.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser and is saved only on your device.
